01 / About & Perspective

Research Premise

Closing the Human-AI Capability Gap

My research examines why organizations invest millions in state-of-the-art foundation models yet struggle with frontline employee adoption. The bottleneck is rarely algorithmic precision; rather, it is cognitive friction, opacity in automated decision-making, and misaligned human incentives.

I build systems that place explainability, telemetry feedback, and human agency at the absolute core of the software architecture.
